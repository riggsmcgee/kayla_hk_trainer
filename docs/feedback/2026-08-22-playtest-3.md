## Ratified decisions (four rounds of `/grilling`, 2026-08-22 → 24)

Every line below is the user's decision. PLAN.md carries the same rows in its own voice.

### The boss — "The Two Bills" (note 1)

**A survival practice tool, not a kill.** The Bills have **no health and cannot be damaged** —
they are *moving furniture*. Her nail bounces off them and nothing else happens; there is no hit
counter in this fight. **One touch ends the run**, like everywhere else in the dojo. The score is
**time survived**. Beat 3 counts as **done at 1:30**; the fight keeps going and keeps escalating
after that, so the thing she chases afterwards is her best time.

**0:00 — Bill the man.** A card reads **BILL THE MAN**, then the fight starts (this replaces the
"Survive a minute…" line). He is **160 px tall** (roster is 52–56; her jump apex is 237, so she
can still clear him). Two attacks:

1. **The lance dash.** He lowers the foam finger like a spear (~0.6 s tell) and crosses the
   **whole arena** fast enough that the ground is not survivable — *there is no safe ground
   anywhere, corners included*. She must be airborne; **pogoing off his head as he passes is the
   intended answer**, and the bounce is a normal straight-up pogo (no sideways carry). He stops
   hard at the far wall and is **stuck ~1 s** — the free window.
2. **The shake-off.** The **first bounce is always free**. If she is still above him ~0.5 s after
   a bounce — a second pogo, or hovering over his head — he swats upward, big and slow (~0.4 s
   tell). The rule is the dojo's usual one: *one hit, then get out.*

**0:30 — Bill the dog.** Everything **freezes**, the arena dims, the dog walks in, and a card
reads **BILL THE DOG** for ~2.5 s (any key skips it). **The clock is paused during the intro** so
the interruption never costs her. Both Bills are on screen from then on. The dog is **slightly
larger than the Knight** (~56–60 px). Two attacks:

1. **Bones** — a 3-shot spread, same shape as the spitter's volley, **pokeable with the nail**.
2. **The roll** — he curls into a ball and bounces around the arena in **parabolic arcs** off the
   floor and walls for ~5 s, then unrolls (Dung Defender-ish). The ball is **pogoable on top and
   damaging on the sides** — deliberately the same vocabulary level 2 taught her with red orbs.

**1:00 — heat.** Both Bills speed up ~25 % and leave less gap between attacks. **No announcement,
no new character**; the speed-up is also what turns Bill's lance dash into **two passes
back-to-back**. One escalation, felt in three places, nothing new to learn at the worst moment.

**HUD:** a clock counting up, with **1:30** shown as the target until she has done it once; her
**best time** below the canvas. The run is recorded like every other run so the bests line works.

**Reveal:** beat 3 stays mysterious until she starts it — the chip keeps saying *"The thing at
the bottom"*, and the Bills only appear when the fight begins.

**Adds during the boss:** none for now. Get the two Bills good first; crowd control later.

**Art:** build with placeholder ink geometry at the correct proportions; the user's painting of
Bill swaps into the renderer later without touching the state machine.

### The gauntlet's waves (note 2) — cut to two, with reinforcements

The well's waves go from three to **two**, then the boss:

- **Wave 1, "the pests": walker + flier** — add a **walker at 0:30**, a **flier at 0:45**.
- **Wave 2, "the real ones": duelist + spitter** — add the **warden at 0:30**, a second
  **duelist at 0:45**.

Reinforcements are **deterministic** (a fixed script per wave, no RNG) and **capped at four
alive**, with a respawning slot counting as alive. The wave's hits requirement and its 60 s do
**not** grow when reinforcements land — the added enemies *are* the difficulty. Every enemy in
the roster still appears, and the warden arriving mid-wave is the "oh no" moment.

So the finale is: **pogo level 4 → wave 1 → wave 2 → the Bills**.

### The duelist (notes 3 and 6) — three attacks, one per thing she can do

- **Lunge** — she walks in. Unchanged.
- **Anti-air: the forward dash-upward swipe** — she jumps in. He **dashes forward while rising**,
  and the hitbox is **much bigger, above his head**, both in geometry and in the drawing. This is
  the fix for note 6: a straight-down pogo chain must no longer be safe. *One hit, then get out*
  — a second bounce eats the swipe.
- **Gap-closer: the leap and diagonal dash** (the user's drawing) — she stands off or runs. He
  leaps to a point high beside her, **hangs a beat and aims at where she is at that pause**, then
  dashes straight down the diagonal. **Longest reach on screen**, ~0.9 s landing recovery — his
  biggest punish window. Triggered by standing beyond ~260 px for ~0.8 s **or** retreating for
  ~0.5 s at any range.
- **Movement: reactive fencing** — faster than today; he advances when she retreats and gives
  ground when she advances, over a light repeating footwork pattern. Deterministic, no RNG.

### The warden (note 4)

A blocked **overhead** hit now provokes a **massive, slow upward attack**: ~0.5 s telegraph, a
tall column above and slightly in front of him, then a recovery of ~1 s during which **the front
is open** before the shield re-aims — long enough for the loop the user described: *hit the
shield → move away → drift back in while falling → land and strike the open front.* A blocked
**front** hit keeps today's riposte, unchanged. Every warden recovery stays open from all sides.

### The Colosseum (note 7)

**Remove both ledges.** The arena is flat, like the well's. Verticality comes from pogoing the
enemies, not from standing somewhere they cannot reach.

### The Pogo Course (notes 9, 10, 12)

- **Level 3 gains red orbs** alongside its drifters.
- **Level 4 gains spike walls** — vertical spikes she must get over (bounce off a red orb, over
  the wall, off the next orb) — **and a spike ceiling** section that forces low bounces on blue
  orbs. **Neither goes in levels 1–3**: those teach one thing each; level 4 is where the course
  turns hostile.
- **Floor spikes stay pogoable** (a real Hollow Knight skill, and level 1's drill 4 teaches it).
  **Spike walls and ceilings are not pogoable** — they are pure hazards.
- **An intro demo per level**, engine-driven on the canvas in the style of the lesson demos:
  L1 bounce off the blue orbs · L2 red hurts and sends you back to the checkpoint · L3 the orbs
  move · L4 walls and ceiling. It plays when a level is freshly opened, **any input skips it**,
  and it **does not replay on a retry**.

### Controls and chrome (notes 8, 11, 13, 14)

- **Z = forward, X = again — on every overlay.** Jump advances (next level / next stage / next
  chapter), attack retries. This includes arena stage clears and fails and the boss. Sessions

- **Scroll to the top on every route change.**
- **"Prove it →" becomes "Next: Pogo Course"** in the gold pill, with the place as small text

- **The chapter strip is secondary**: it moves to the **very bottom** of the page, small, above

### Round 5 — decisions the code review surfaced

After the interview closed, five read-only scouts mapped the decisions onto the code and found
seven branches the interview never visited. Settled 2026-08-24:

- **No free bounce on the duelist.** Any approach from above is punished — the rising swipe
  covers the pogo apex, so a jump-in is never safe on its own. **The way in is to bait him:**
  fake the jump so he commits to the anti-air and then land a forward slash into its recovery,
  or draw the forward dash and pogo him while he is stuck. This is a deliberate exception to the
  "one hit, then get out" rule the Bills and the warden follow — the duelist is the enemy that
  teaches *provoke, dodge, punish*, so his head is never free real estate.
- **Fail overlay:** both keys retry (there is no "forward" from a fail); the copy names X only, so
  no key is dead.
- **The 2 s stage-clear banner:** Z advances, the banner expires on its own, and **X does nothing**
  there — replaying from the banner could double-fire the cleared callback and record a second run.
  X is "again" on every other overlay.
- **Level 4 bests reset** when the level grows (walls and ceiling take it from ~4300 to ~5000 px).
  A best she cannot beat is worse than no best. Silent — no copy about it.
- **The warden's head stays covered during the skyward telegraph.** A downslash rings off the
  shield and does not interrupt the attack: "the shield covers one side at a time" is the rule she
  has learned, and it should not develop exceptions.
- **Reading Enemies copy:** the third duelist attack is **not** documented — she discovers it in
  play. But the page must not assert something false, so the line claiming he has *two* answers is
  cut rather than extended.
- **Wave 2 gets a survivability bot** — a headless dodge-only floor test proving the wave can be
  survived for 60 s, the arena's equivalent of the Pogo Course's completability bot. Shipping an
  impossible wave would cost a whole playtest round.

Resolved from the existing ratified text, no new decision needed: the finale needs the boss to
count as done (1:30 is explicit); **Bill's head is reachable** because the 70 px nail out-reaches
his body exactly as the duelist's does today — she bounces before touching him, and falling onto
him without slashing is a touch, which is the lesson; telegraph durations do **not** scale with the
1:00 heat (the tell is the fairness contract); and a player who cleared old waves 1–2 keeps them
when the count drops to two.

## Causes found in the code (before the interview; no decision needed for these)

- **Note 5, dash trail:** `stepPlayer` decrements `dashTimer` only while `dashTimer > 1e-9`
  (`web/src/engine/player.ts`); 15 steps of 1/60 from 0.25 leave `4.9e-17`, never 0, so the
  branch stops running and `drawKnight` (`render.ts`, gate `dashTimer > 0`) draws the streak
  forever — until a new dash resets the timer (or a course respawn zeroes it; the arena never
  respawns, hence "until the next dash"). Fix: snap to 0 at/below the epsilon, or gate the
  renderer on the same epsilon.
- **Note 6, anti-air:** `enemyAttackHitbox` for the anti-air is a 68×52 box whose top is
  104 px above the duelist's feet. A pogo connects when the 70 px down-nail reaches the top of
  his 52 px body, i.e. feet at 111–122 px — always *above* the swipe — then rises 120 px. The
  attack cycle (0.35 telegraph + 0.25 active + 0.55 recovery + 0.6 cooldown = 1.75 s) is three
  times a bounce. Geometry, not luck: a straight-down pogo chain cannot be hit.
- **Note 4, warden:** a blocked *overhead* hit provokes today's horizontal riposte toward
  whichever side she's on (`resolveNailHit`); its hitbox is only ~52 px tall, so it whiffs
  under a Knight who is above him.
- **Note 8, scroll:** HashRouter with no `ScrollRestoration`/`scrollTo` anywhere in `web/src`.
- **Note 7, platforms:** the Colosseum ledges sit 130 px above the floor; walker can't climb,
  duelist's lunge needs |dy| < 60 and its swipe reaches 104 px, the warden's bash box is ~50 px
  tall — three of five enemies cannot touch a Knight on a ledge, and the hunting property test
  only asserts "paces beneath her" for that case.
- **Note 2, reinforcements:** `StageDef` has no time schedule; enemies are spawned once at
  stage load (`dodgeArenaSession.ts`). Mid-stage spawns need a schedule field + a clock.
- **Note 10, spike walls:** spikes are plain AABBs (a tall one collides fine) but `drawSpikes`
  slices along x and draws upward teeth only, and `LevelSpec` can only express pits. Walls and
  ceilings need a direction on the spike spec and a renderer case; a ceiling is a `solids` box.
- **Note 11, Z/X:** sessions know `jumpKey` but have no attack-key name; overlays restart on
  the raw jump press only; the page's "Next level" is a DOM button.
